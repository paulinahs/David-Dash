David Dash

Run, jump and strike the marble blocks from below. Every block gives up a face of David. Reach the statue at the end of the gallery.

← → run  ·  Space or ↑ jump (hold to leap higher)  ·  P pause  ·  M sound. Click the game first if keys do nothing.

Michelangelo carved David from a single block of Carrara marble between 1501 and 1504. He now stands in the Galleria dell'Accademia in Florence. The pigeons are not part of the original commission.
